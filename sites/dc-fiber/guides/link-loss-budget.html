Building a fiber link loss budget

Add up the losses and compare with what the optics can handle.

A loss budget estimates how much light the link will lose so you know before testing whether it should work.

Add up

ItemTypical allowance (check your standard and vendor specs)
Each mated connector pair0.75 dB max per TIA-568 (low-loss connectors are often much less)
Each splice0.3 dB max
Multimode fiber at 850 nmabout 3.0–3.5 dB per km
Single-mode fiber at 1310 nmabout 0.4–1.0 dB per km

Compare

The total must be below the maximum channel loss of the application, such as a particular Ethernet speed and optic. High-speed multimode links have very tight budgets, so every connector counts.

Then test

Tier 1 testing with a light source and power meter measures total loss. Tier 2 adds an OTDR trace to locate events. Record results for every link.
