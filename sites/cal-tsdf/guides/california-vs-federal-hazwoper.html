California vs federal HAZWOPER: what's different?

Why Cal/OSHA courses exist.

California is a state-plan state. Cal/OSHA's HAZWOPER rule is Title 8 §5192, and it must be at least as effective as the federal rule.

In practice

  • The training hours are the same: 40, 24 and 8-hour refreshers
  • California's hazardous waste definitions are broader than federal RCRA, so more materials count as hazardous waste
  • California has its own references, agencies and reporting (Cal/OSHA, DTSC, CUPAs)

Which course?

Employers in California often prefer training that cites Title 8 and California rules. See Cal/OSHA 40-Hour HAZWOPER Training (2026) and Cal/OSHA 8-Hour HAZWOPER Refresher Training.
