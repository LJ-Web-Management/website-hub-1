RCRA Universal Waste Handler Training

Batteries, lamps, mercury devices and aerosol cans under 40 CFR 273.

Healthcare facilities produce lots of universal waste: batteries from devices, fluorescent lamps, mercury equipment and aerosol cans. This two-hour course covers handling, labels and the one-year limit.

What you'll cover

  • Universal waste categories
  • Labeling and accumulation
  • Broken lamps and mercury spills
  • Shipping

Who it's for

Facilities, biomed, environmental services and materials management staff.
