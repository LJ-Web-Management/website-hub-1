BMS, BAS, EPMS and DCIM: who does what?

Four acronyms, overlapping jobs. A quick map.

SystemWatches and controlsMain users
BMS / BAS (building management or automation system)HVAC, chillers, air handlers, sometimes lighting and fire interfacesFacilities, mechanical
EPMS (electrical power monitoring system)Switchgear, UPS, generators, PDUs, power qualityElectrical, critical facilities
DCIM (data center infrastructure management)Assets, rack space, power and cooling capacity, connectivity, sometimes pulling BMS and EPMS dataCapacity planning, operations, IT

How they fit

The BMS and EPMS run and protect equipment in real time. DCIM sits above them, combining their data with asset and capacity information for planning.

Integration tips

  • Agree on point naming before integration
  • Decide which system is the source of truth for each data point
  • Secure the networks: building systems are a cyber target too

See Building Automation Systems (BAS) Fundamentals for BAS fundamentals and DCIM Platform Fundamentals for DCIM.
