Building Automation Systems (BAS) Fundamentals

Understand the building automation systems that tie HVAC, power, and monitoring together.

Building Automation Systems (BAS) are the control layer behind HVAC, lighting, and power monitoring in modern facilities — and increasingly overlap with the BMS and DCIM platforms used in data centers. This course covers BAS architecture, sensor and controller basics, and how automation logic actually gets built and tuned. You'll leave able to read a points list, understand a control sequence, and troubleshoot the integration issues that come up when BAS meets facility-specific monitoring.

What you'll cover

  • BAS Architecture Fundamentals
  • Control Sequences & Points Lists
  • Sensor & Controller Basics
  • BAS Integration & Troubleshooting

Who it's for

Facilities engineers and technicians working with or evaluating BAS platforms.
