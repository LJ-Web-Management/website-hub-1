OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

Awareness-level training for operators and staff who may discover a release.

Most plant employees will never respond to a release, but many could be the first to see one. Awareness training teaches them to recognize it, get clear and raise the alarm.

What you'll cover

  • Recognizing a release
  • Identifying the material
  • Isolating the area
  • Raising the alarm

Who it's for

Operators, maintenance, warehouse and security staff.
