Environmental rules that apply to data centers

A quick map of the federal programs most sites run into.

Exact requirements depend on your site, state and local rules. These are the federal programs that come up most often.

TopicProgramWhat it covers
GeneratorsClean Air Act (state air permits, EPA engine rules)Permits, run-hour limits, emissions, recordkeeping
Diesel storageSPCC (40 CFR 112)Oil spill prevention plans above storage thresholds
Fuel tanksUST rules (40 CFR 280) for underground tanksLeak detection, corrosion protection
RefrigerantsClean Air Act Section 608Technician certification, leak repair, recordkeeping
Batteries, lampsUniversal waste (40 CFR 273)Labeling, accumulation time, shipping
Other wastesRCRAWaste determination and generator status
Chemicals on siteEPCRA Tier IIAnnual reporting of stored chemicals (batteries and fuel often trigger it)
StormwaterClean Water Act permitsConstruction and some industrial sites

Who owns it?

Assign an owner for each program, keep a compliance calendar, and train the people whose daily work creates the records.
