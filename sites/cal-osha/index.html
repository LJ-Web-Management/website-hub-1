Questions

Is my federal 40-hour valid in California?

Generally, yes. The hours match, and Cal/OSHA looks at whether your training covered the 5192 requirements. Taking your next refresher as a Cal/OSHA course fills in the California differences. More here.

Do public employees need HAZWOPER in California?

Yes. Cal/OSHA covers state and local government workers, so city and county crews who work hazardous waste sites or respond to releases fall under 8 CCR 5192.

Does the online course cover the field days?

The course covers the classroom hours. The supervised field experience (three days for the 40-hour, one for the 24-hour) is done on the job with your employer.

Can I enroll my whole crew?

Yes. Set the number of seats in checkout and pay once, or call +1-866-429-6742 for invoicing.
