Cal/OSHA 24 hour HAZWOPER TSDF RCRA Training

The 24-hour initial training for California treatment, storage and disposal facility workers.

Workers at permitted hazardous waste treatment, storage and disposal facilities fall under 8 CCR 5192(p), with 24 hours of initial training. California TSDFs are also permitted by DTSC under Title 22, so this course covers both the Cal/OSHA worker-safety side and the hazardous waste rules that shape daily work.

What you'll cover

  • 8 CCR 5192(p) requirements for TSD facilities
  • DTSC permits and Title 22 operating requirements
  • Container and tank management
  • Waste receipt, sampling and profiling
  • Facility emergency response plan
  • PPE, monitoring and decon at a TSDF

Who it's for

Operators and technicians at California hazardous waste TSD facilities.
