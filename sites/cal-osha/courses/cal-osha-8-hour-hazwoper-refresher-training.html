Cal/OSHA 8-Hour HAZWOPER Refresher Training

The annual 8-hour HAZWOPER refresher, written to Cal/OSHA Title 8.

8 CCR 5192(e)(8) requires eight hours of refresher training every year. This refresher reviews the core HAZWOPER topics with California references, so the certificate lines up with what a Cal/OSHA inspector will look for.

What you'll cover

  • Title 8 HAZWOPER refresher review
  • Updates to Cal/OSHA rules and exposure limits
  • Heat illness prevention on hazardous waste sites
  • PPE and respirator refresher
  • Decon and emergency procedures
  • California incident lessons learned

Who it's for

California workers with a Cal/OSHA or federal 40- or 24-hour HAZWOPER certificate.
