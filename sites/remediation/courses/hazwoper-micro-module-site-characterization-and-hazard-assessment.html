HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Site Characterization & Hazard Assessment

A 20-minute module on how sites are evaluated before and during work.

Before anyone goes in, the site is characterized. This module explains the preliminary evaluation, IDLH conditions and how monitoring keeps the picture up to date.

What you'll cover

  • Preliminary evaluation
  • Recognizing IDLH conditions
  • Site history and records
  • Ongoing monitoring

Who it's for
