OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training

Using multi-gas meters and PIDs to stay out of H2S, LEL and low-oxygen atmospheres.

Tank batteries, pits, cellars and vaults can hold H2S, hydrocarbon vapors and low oxygen. This two-hour course covers what your monitor measures, bump tests and calibration, and the readings that mean you leave now.

What you'll cover

  • Oxygen, LEL, H2S and CO sensors
  • PIDs for hydrocarbon vapors
  • Bump test vs calibration
  • Alarm set points
  • Monitoring before entering cellars, pits and tanks

Who it's for

Anyone who carries a personal gas monitor in the field.
