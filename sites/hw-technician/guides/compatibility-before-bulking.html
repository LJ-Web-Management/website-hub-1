Compatibility testing before bulking

Why you test before you pour drums together.

Bulking (combining compatible waste from many containers into one) saves money but can cause fires, toxic gases or explosions if wastes react.

Before you bulk

  • Check profiles, labels and SDSs
  • Run field compatibility tests on small samples: pH, water reactivity, oxidizer, flammability and others
  • Mix small amounts first and watch for heat, gas, bubbling or color change

When bulking

  • Ground and bond containers for flammable liquids
  • Use proper PPE and respiratory protection
  • Work with air monitoring and ventilation
  • Have spill and fire equipment ready

Never

Bulk unknowns, or mix incompatible waste streams to "make them disappear." Decon your equipment after each job (HAZWOPER Micro-Module: Decontamination Procedures).
