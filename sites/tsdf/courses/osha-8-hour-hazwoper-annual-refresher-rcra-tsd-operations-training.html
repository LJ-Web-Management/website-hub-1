OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Annual Refresher - RCRA TSD Operations Training

The annual 8-hour refresher for TSD facility workers under 1910.120(p).

Paragraph (p) requires eight hours of refresher training every year for TSD workers. This course reviews the core topics and focuses on what changes and what goes wrong at TSD facilities.

What you'll cover

  • Review of (p) program elements
  • Waste handling incidents and lessons learned
  • PPE and monitoring review
  • Emergency response plan review
  • Regulatory updates

Who it's for

TSD facility workers who completed the 24-hour initial training.
