OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Supervisor Refresher Training

The annual 8-hour refresher for HAZWOPER supervisors.

Supervisors need eight hours of refresher training a year, the same as their crews. This version keeps the supervisor's perspective: the programs, the paperwork and the judgment calls.

What you'll cover

  • Refresher review of HAZWOPER core topics
  • Site safety and health plan upkeep
  • Briefings and tailgate meetings
  • Monitoring results and action levels
  • Medical surveillance and recordkeeping
  • Case studies in site leadership

Who it's for

Supervisors who completed the 8-hour HAZWOPER supervisor course.
