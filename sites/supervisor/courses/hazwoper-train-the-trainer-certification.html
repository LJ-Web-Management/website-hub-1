HAZWOPER Train-the-Trainer Certification

Become a qualified in-house HAZWOPER trainer for your organization.

29 CFR 1910.120(e)(5) requires that HAZWOPER trainers be qualified to teach the subject, through a training program or academic credentials plus instructional experience. This 24-hour course covers both the HAZWOPER content and how to teach it to adults on a work site.

What you'll cover

  • Trainer qualifications under (e)(5)
  • How adults learn on the job
  • Building lesson plans for the 40, 24 and 8-hour courses
  • Running hands-on and field exercises
  • Testing and demonstrating competency
  • Certificates and training records

Who it's for

EHS managers, safety coordinators and experienced site supervisors who'll train their own crews.
