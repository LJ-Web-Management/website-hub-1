Data Center Design Fundamentals

Design data center facilities that scale — from site selection to redundancy strategy.

Every high-availability data center starts as a set of design decisions — and the wrong ones are expensive to unwind after concrete is poured. This course walks through the full design lifecycle used by enterprise facility teams: international standards (ANSI/TIA-942, ISO 14644), site selection criteria, thermal design principles, power architecture, and the redundancy trade-offs (N, N+1, N+2, 2N) that determine both uptime and capital cost. You'll work through real design case studies, thermal modeling concepts, and ROI analysis frameworks used to justify facility investment to executive stakeholders. Rather than teaching a single vendor's playbook, the course focuses on the trade-off reasoning behind design choices, so you can evaluate proposals, vet vendor recommendations, and defend your own design decisions with data. By the end, you'll be able to read a facility design proposal critically, model redundancy options against budget constraints, and build the financial case for an expansion or new build.

What you'll cover

  • Standards & International Best Practices (ANSI/TIA-942, ISO 14644)
  • Site Selection & Facility Planning
  • Thermal Design Principles
  • Power Architecture Overview
  • Redundancy Models & Trade-offs
  • Financial Modeling for Expansion

Who it's for

Facility engineers, design consultants, and project leads planning new builds or major expansions.
