Data Center Operations Management

Run daily operations like the teams behind Tier 3/4 facilities — maintenance, incident response, and SLAs.

Great facility design means little without operational discipline behind it. This program covers the full operations lifecycle: DCIM asset tracking, preventive maintenance planning, emergency response protocols, SLA metric development, and energy optimization — the systems that keep a well-designed facility actually performing. The course includes lab simulations of critical failure scenarios and DCIM platform training concepts, so the frameworks aren't just theoretical. You'll build maintenance checklists, incident response runbooks, and SLA scorecards you can adapt directly to your facility. Drawing on real-world case studies from Tier 3/4 operations teams, this course is built for the person who owns uptime — not just the systems that support it.

What you'll cover

  • Facility Lifecycle Management
  • DCIM Systems & Asset Tracking
  • Preventive Maintenance Planning
  • Emergency Response & Incident Management
  • SLA Metrics & Performance Tracking
  • Energy Management & Efficiency Optimization

Who it's for

Facility managers and operations directors responsible for day-to-day uptime.
