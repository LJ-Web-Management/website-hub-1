Four moves, every spill.

  1. 01StopShut the valve, upright the drum, kill the pump. Only if it's safe.
  2. 02ContainCover the drain first, then ring the spill with socks or boom.
  3. 03Clean upAbsorb from the edges in, bag it, label it as waste.
  4. 04ReportTell your supervisor. Large releases may need agency reporting.
