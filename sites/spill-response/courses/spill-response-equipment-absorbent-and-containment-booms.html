Spill Response Equipment - Absorbent & Containment Booms

Using absorbent and containment booms to stop spills on land and water.

Booms are the line between a contained spill and an environmental release. This one-hour course explains the difference between absorbent and containment booms, how to deploy and connect them, and how to keep them working in current, wind and rain.

What you'll cover

  • Absorbent vs containment booms
  • Deploying booms on land, in ditches and on water
  • Connecting and anchoring boom sections
  • Current, wind and wave limits
  • Protecting drains and outfalls
  • Recovery and disposal

Who it's for

Fuel terminal staff, marina and dock workers, industrial facilities near waterways and spill response technicians.
