OSHA Spill Prevention and Response Safety Training

Spill prevention and first response for oil and chemical handlers under OSHA and EPA SPCC rules.

Most spills are small, and most become big because the first person on scene didn't know what to do. This two-hour course teaches facility staff how spills happen, how to prevent them and how to respond safely to the ones that still occur. It covers the training topics EPA's SPCC rule expects for oil-handling personnel.

What you'll cover

  • Common causes of spills at facilities
  • SPCC plans and secondary containment
  • Assessing a spill: is it safe to respond?
  • Stop, contain, clean up, report
  • PPE for incidental spill response
  • Reporting thresholds and who to call

Who it's for

Oil-handling personnel, warehouse and maintenance staff, fuel and chemical transfer operators.
