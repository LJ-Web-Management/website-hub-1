Bump test vs calibration

Two checks, two jobs. Both matter.

Bump testCalibration
PurposeConfirms sensors respond and alarms workAdjusts the meter to read accurately against a known gas
WhenBefore each day's use (common industry practice and many manufacturers' recommendation)On the manufacturer's schedule, or when a bump test fails
GasKnown concentration, brieflyKnown concentration, until readings stabilize
ResultPass or failMeter adjusted

Record both

Keep records of every bump test and calibration. If an incident happens, they show the meter was working.

If a bump test fails

Calibrate and bump again. If it still fails, take the meter out of service. See OSHA Air Monitoring and Gas Detection Training.
