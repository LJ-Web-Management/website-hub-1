Capacity Planning & Forecasting

Build a 3–5 year capacity plan before you run out of power, cooling, or space.

Strategic planning for growth: workload characterization, power and cooling demand forecasting, and infrastructure constraint analysis. This course gives you the frameworks to see capacity constraints coming years before they become emergencies. You'll build phased expansion plans and financial models, and finish with the ability to develop 3–5 year capacity plans and growth scenarios your leadership team can actually plan around.

What you'll cover

  • Workload Characterization
  • Power & Cooling Demand Forecasting
  • Constraint Analysis
  • Expansion Planning Frameworks

Who it's for

Facility and IT planners responsible for growth forecasting.
