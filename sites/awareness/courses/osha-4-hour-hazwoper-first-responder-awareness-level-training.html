OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

Awareness level training: recognize a hazardous release, isolate the area and notify.

Under 29 CFR 1910.120(q)(6)(i), first responders at the awareness level are people likely to witness or discover a hazardous substance release. Their job is to start the response by notifying the right people, and then take no further action. This four-hour course teaches exactly that.

What you'll cover

  • What hazardous substances are
  • Recognizing a release
  • Identifying materials with placards, labels and the ERG
  • Your role in the emergency response plan
  • Isolating the area
  • Notifying the right people

Who it's for

Security officers, warehouse staff, drivers, receptionists and anyone who may discover a release.
