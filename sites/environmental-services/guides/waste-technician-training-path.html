The waste technician training path

From new hire to lead tech: the certificates field techs usually build up, in order.

Environmental services companies hire people with all kinds of backgrounds. The training path tends to look the same.

Year one

TrainingWhy
40-hour HAZWOPERField work on customer sites and cleanups
EPA RCRA/DOT Combined Hazardous Materials Compliance TrainingYou'll be packaging, marking and loading hazmat within weeks
OSHA HAZWOPER and RCRA Hazardous Waste Management Technician Safety TrainingThe technician skills: lab packs, consolidation, profiles
Respirator fit test and medical clearanceBefore respirator use

If you'll also work at the company's permitted facility, add OSHA 24-Hour HAZWOPER - RCRA TSD Operations Training.

Every year

  • 8-hour HAZWOPER refresher, and OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER Annual Refresher - RCRA TSD Operations Training if you work at the TSDF
  • Respirator fit test
  • Medical surveillance exam, if you're covered

Every three years

  • DOT hazmat refresher

Moving up to lead tech

  • 8-hour HAZWOPER supervisor training
  • Hazardous Waste Manifest Training if you'll prepare manifests for customers
  • Incident command if you'll lead emergency response jobs

Keep your own copies

Your certificates are your résumé in this industry. Keep copies on your phone and in a folder. When you change companies, the right paperwork can save you weeks of retraining.
