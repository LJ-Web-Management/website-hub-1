OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER (q) Incident Command Training

Training for the person who takes charge under 1910.120(q)(3).

The senior emergency response official to arrive becomes the individual in charge of a site-specific incident command system. This eight-hour course trains that person to set up command, size up the hazards, assign roles and run a safe response.

What you'll cover

  • ICS structure and roles
  • Size-up and hazard identification
  • Choosing PPE and entry teams
  • The buddy system and backup personnel
  • Safety officer authority
  • Decon, termination and critique

Who it's for

Shift supervisors, hazmat team leaders, fire officers and plant emergency coordinators.
