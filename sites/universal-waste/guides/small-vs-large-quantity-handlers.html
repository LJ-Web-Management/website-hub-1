Small vs large quantity handlers of universal waste

The 5,000 kg line and what changes when you cross it.

Small quantity handlerLarge quantity handler
Accumulates at any one timeLess than 5,000 kg total5,000 kg or more
EPA notification and ID numberNot requiredRequired
TrainingInform employees of handling and emergency proceduresEnsure employees are thoroughly familiar with proper handling and emergency procedures
Shipment recordsNot requiredKeep records of shipments received and sent

Both

  • Time limit: up to one year from when the waste was generated or received
  • Labels: for example "Universal Waste—Batteries" or "Used Lamps"
  • Show the date: on the container, an inventory, or another method
  • Contain releases and manage broken items properly
  • Ship only to another handler or a destination facility

Many states add their own categories, such as electronics, so check your state.
