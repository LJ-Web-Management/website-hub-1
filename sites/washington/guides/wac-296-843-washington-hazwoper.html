WAC 296-843: Washington's HAZWOPER rule

Washington writes its own HAZWOPER rule. What's the same as federal OSHA and what to watch for.

Washington is a state-plan state. Its Department of Labor and Industries (L&I), through the Division of Occupational Safety and Health (DOSH), enforces workplace safety. Washington's HAZWOPER rule is chapter 296-843 WAC.

What's the same

The training structure follows the federal rule:

  • 40 hours plus 3 days of supervised field experience for general site workers
  • 24 hours plus 1 day for limited-exposure workers
  • 8 additional hours for supervisors
  • 8 hours of refresher training every year
  • Emergency response training by responder level

What's different

  • Numbering and wording. Washington organized the rule its own way, so citations look different.
  • Related rules. Respiratory protection, confined spaces and other related standards are in Washington's own chapters.
  • Public employees. As a state plan, Washington covers state and local government workers.

Training approach

A federal 40-hour course covers the same core training. For the annual refresher, 8 Hour HAZWOPER Refresher - State-Plan Versions Available covers the state-plan context. Keep certificates with your company's written HAZWOPER program, since L&I inspectors will want to see both.
