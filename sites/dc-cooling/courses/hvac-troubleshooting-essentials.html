HVAC Systems Troubleshooting Essentials

Hands-on troubleshooting skills for CRAC/CRAH systems and common cooling failures.

Built for technicians who need practical troubleshooting skills, not design theory. This course covers refrigeration cycle fundamentals, psychrometrics basics, and hands-on CRAC/CRAH diagnostics — the skills you use during an actual cooling failure, not just a scheduled inspection. You'll work through common failure modes, airflow measurement techniques, and a preventive maintenance schedule you can apply immediately, plus emergency troubleshooting procedures for when cooling capacity drops unexpectedly.

What you'll cover

  • Refrigeration Cycle Fundamentals
  • CRAC/CRAH Diagnostics
  • Common Failures & Troubleshooting
  • Maintenance Best Practices

Who it's for

HVAC technicians working on data center cooling equipment.
