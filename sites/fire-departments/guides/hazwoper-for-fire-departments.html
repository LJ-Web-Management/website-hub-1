HAZWOPER for fire departments: which level does each member need?

OSHA's responder levels, applied to a fire department roster.

Fire departments are emergency responders under HAZWOPER paragraph (q). In state-plan states the state rule applies to public employees. In federal OSHA states, EPA's 40 CFR Part 311 applies the same requirements to public employees. Volunteer coverage depends on the state.

Many departments also train to NFPA's hazmat competency standard (now NFPA 470), which lines up with OSHA's levels.

Operations level

OSHA requires at least 8 hours of training, or demonstrated competency, for first responders at the operations level. Most departments deliver it in-house with hands-on drills.

Annual refresher

Responders need annual refresher training, or a documented demonstration of competency, under (q)(8). See 8 Hour HAZWOPER and Incident Command Refresher Training.
