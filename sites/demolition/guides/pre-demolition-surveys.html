Pre-demolition surveys and hazardous materials removal

What has to come out of a building before the excavator goes in.

Before a building comes down, hazardous materials have to be found and removed. Missing them puts the crew at risk and can turn a whole debris pile into hazardous waste.

Required by law

Asbestos: the federal asbestos NESHAP (40 CFR Part 61, Subpart M) requires a thorough inspection for asbestos before demolition of regulated facilities, and removal of regulated asbestos-containing material first.

Commonly found

MaterialWhere
AsbestosInsulation, floor tile, roofing, transite, mastics
Lead paintSteel structures, older interiors
PCBsLight ballasts, transformers, some caulks and paints
MercuryThermostats, switches, fluorescent lamps
RefrigerantsHVAC and refrigeration equipment
Process residuesTanks, pipes, pits and sumps in industrial buildings
Universal wasteLamps and batteries

Separate rules apply

Asbestos (29 CFR 1926.1101) and lead (1926.62) have their own OSHA standards and training. HAZWOPER applies when the demolition is part of a hazardous waste cleanup, or when crews handle hazardous waste and process residues on a contaminated site.

Order of work

  1. Survey
  2. Remove and dispose of hazardous materials
  3. Clean and decontaminate process equipment
  4. Demolish
  5. Manage soil and debris under the site plan
