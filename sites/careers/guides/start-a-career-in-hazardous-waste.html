How to start a career in hazardous waste and environmental cleanup

The jobs, the training and the first steps for people new to the field.

Environmental services is a field where training matters more than a degree for many entry-level jobs.

Where the jobs are

Employer typeEntry-level roles
Remediation contractorsField technician, laborer, equipment operator
Environmental services / waste companiesWaste technician, driver, facility operator
Industrial cleaning companiesVac truck operator, technician
Emergency response contractorsResponse technician
Consulting firmsField technician, sampler

Training that helps you get hired

  1. OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER Training: the certificate most postings ask for
  2. A driver's license, and for some roles a CDL with hazmat endorsement
  3. EPA RCRA/DOT Combined Hazardous Materials Compliance Training for waste technician and driver roles
  4. Basic first aid/CPR

Your first months

Employers provide site orientation, the supervised field days required after the 40-hour, respirator fit testing and medical clearance. Show up on time, ask questions and follow the site safety plan.

Keep it current

Take the 8-hour refresher every year to stay employable between jobs.
