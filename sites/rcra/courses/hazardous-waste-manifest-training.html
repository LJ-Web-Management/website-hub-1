Hazardous Waste Manifest Training

How to prepare, sign and track the Uniform Hazardous Waste Manifest and e-Manifest.

The person who signs a hazardous waste manifest certifies, under penalty of law, that the waste is properly described, packaged, marked and labeled. This two-hour course teaches the manifest box by box and covers e-Manifest, exception reports and record retention.

What you'll cover

  • EPA Form 8700-22, box by box
  • The generator's certification statement
  • e-Manifest and paper copies
  • DOT shipping descriptions on the manifest
  • Tracking returns and exception reports
  • Recordkeeping

Who it's for

Anyone who prepares or signs hazardous waste manifests: EHS staff, shipping and receiving, plant managers.
