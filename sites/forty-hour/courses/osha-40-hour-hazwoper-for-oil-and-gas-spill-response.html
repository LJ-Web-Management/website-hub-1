OSHA 40 Hour HAZWOPER for Oil & Gas Spill Response

The 40-hour initial HAZWOPER for crews who respond to crude, fuel and produced-water spills.

Oil spill response is a hazardous waste operation. This track focuses on what a spill crew sees: benzene and H2S exposure, flammable atmospheres, shoreline and land cleanup and waste you'll be loading out for days.

What you'll cover

  • Crude and refined product hazards, including benzene and H2S
  • Flammable atmospheres and LEL monitoring
  • Booming, skimming and land-based recovery
  • Shoreline and soil cleanup
  • Heat stress and long-shift fatigue
  • Oily waste handling and decon

Who it's for

Oil spill response organization (OSRO) crews, oilfield service workers and pipeline and terminal response teams.
