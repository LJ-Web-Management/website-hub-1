A battery maintenance schedule that catches failures early

What to check monthly, quarterly and yearly on UPS batteries.

One weak battery can take a whole string down. Regular checks find it first. Always follow the manufacturer's schedule and IEEE guidance for your battery type.

Monthly

  • Visual inspection: swelling, leaks, corrosion, cracked cases
  • Room temperature (heat shortens life fast)
  • Overall string float voltage and charger output

Quarterly

  • Individual cell or block voltages
  • Internal resistance or impedance, trended over time
  • Terminal temperatures with an infrared camera

Yearly

  • Connection resistance and torque
  • Load or capacity test as the manufacturer and your program require

Replace when

Capacity falls below about 80% of rated, resistance rises sharply against its baseline, or the case is damaged. Learn the DC basics in Batteries and DC Circuits.
