Heat stress in chemical protective clothing

Why PPE changes the heat math, and how to protect crews.

Your body cools mostly by sweating. Impermeable suits stop sweat from evaporating, so heat builds up quickly, even on a mild day.

Signs to watch

ConditionSignsAction
Heat rash, crampsMuscle spasms, red bumpsRest, water, cool place
Heat exhaustionHeavy sweating, weakness, dizziness, nausea, headacheRemove from heat, remove PPE after decon, cool down, fluids, get medical help if not improving
Heat strokeConfusion, slurred speech, hot skin, collapse, seizuresEmergency: call 911, cool the worker immediately

Controls

  • Acclimatize new and returning workers gradually
  • Work/rest cycles based on heat, workload and PPE level
  • Cooling vests and shaded, cool rest areas
  • Hydration: small amounts often
  • Physiological monitoring: heart rate and body temperature as your site plan specifies
  • Buddy system to spot early signs
