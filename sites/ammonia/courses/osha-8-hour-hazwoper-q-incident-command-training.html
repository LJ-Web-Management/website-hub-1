OSHA 8 Hour HAZWOPER (q) Incident Command Training

Incident command for the person who takes charge of an ammonia release.

When an ammonia release goes beyond incidental, someone has to command it. This eight-hour course trains supervisors to set up incident command and work with the fire department.

What you'll cover

  • Incident command structure
  • Size-up and isolation zones
  • Entry teams and backup
  • Coordination with fire services

Who it's for

Refrigeration supervisors, plant managers and response team leaders.
