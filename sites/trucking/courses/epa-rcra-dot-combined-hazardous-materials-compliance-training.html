EPA RCRA/DOT Combined Hazardous Materials Compliance Training

DOT hazmat employee training combined with RCRA hazardous waste rules.

Drivers who haul hazmat are hazmat employees under 49 CFR 172.704. Waste haulers also need to know RCRA transporter duties. This eight-hour course covers both.

What you'll cover

  • Hazmat classes and placards
  • Shipping papers
  • Packaging and securement basics
  • Hazardous waste and the manifest
  • Security awareness

Who it's for

Hazmat drivers, dispatchers and waste haulers.
