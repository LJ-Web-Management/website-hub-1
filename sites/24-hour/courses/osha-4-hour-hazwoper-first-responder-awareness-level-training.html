OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training

First responder awareness training for staff who may discover a release but won't work on a waste site.

If your people don't work on a hazardous waste site at all but might see a release, they may need awareness-level training under 1910.120(q)(6)(i) instead of the 24-hour.

What you'll cover

  • Recognizing a release
  • Identifying the material
  • Securing the area
  • Notifying the right people

Who it's for

Security, warehouse and facility staff who may witness a hazardous substance release.
