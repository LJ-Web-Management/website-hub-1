Chemical spill response on campus: who does what

Lab staff, custodians, campus police and EHS each have a role. Define them before the spill.

A spill in a teaching lab at 9 p.m. tests whether everyone knows their role.

Typical roles

WhoRole
Lab personnelClean up small spills of known materials they're trained for, with the lab's spill kit. Otherwise evacuate and call
Custodians and building staffRecognize, keep people away, call. Don't clean up chemical spills
Campus policeSecure the area, help evacuate, call EHS and the fire department
EHSAssess, decide on cleanup or outside help, document
Fire department / hazmat contractorLarger or unknown releases

Incidental vs emergency

Small, known spills that the people in the area can safely handle are incidental. Anything bigger, unknown or dangerous is an emergency: evacuate and call.

Training

  • Custodians and police: OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training
  • Lab staff: lab-specific spill training plus the spill kit
  • EHS and emergency coordinators: Hazardous Waste Contingency Plan and Emergency Coordinator Training if you're an LQG

After the spill

Spill cleanup materials are often hazardous waste. Bag, label and send them to the waste program.
