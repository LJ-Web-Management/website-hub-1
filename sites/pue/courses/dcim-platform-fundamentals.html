DCIM Platform Fundamentals

Understand DCIM strategy and build the ROI case for implementation.

Platform-agnostic DCIM training: software-based infrastructure management, asset tracking, power/thermal modeling, and data visualization. Rather than teaching one vendor's tool, this course focuses on DCIM strategy — what to look for and why. You'll finish with a vendor evaluation framework and the ability to build the ROI business case for implementing a DCIM system in your own facility.

What you'll cover

  • DCIM Architecture & Benefits
  • Asset Inventory & Tracking
  • Power & Thermal Modeling
  • Reporting & Analytics
  • Vendor Evaluation & Selection

Facility and IT managers evaluating or implementing a DCIM platform.
