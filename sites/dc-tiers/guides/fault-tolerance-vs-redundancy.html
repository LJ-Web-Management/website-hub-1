Fault tolerance vs redundancy

Why Tier IV is more than 'more equipment'.

Redundancy means having spares. Fault tolerance means a failure anywhere doesn't reach the IT load, without anyone having to act.

What Tier IV adds

  • Simultaneously active paths, not a standby path waiting to be switched in
  • Compartmentalization: paths physically separated so a fire or flood in one room can't take out both
  • Continuous cooling, so a power transfer doesn't let temperatures spike
  • Autonomous response: the system rides through a failure on its own

The trade-offs

Fault tolerance costs capital, space and efficiency. Many organizations instead spread workloads across multiple Tier III sites and rely on disaster recovery (Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity).
