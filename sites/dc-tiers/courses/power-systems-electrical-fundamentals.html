Power Systems & Electrical Fundamentals

Master data center electrical infrastructure — from utility interconnection to UPS and PDU architecture.

Power failures are the fastest way to take a data center offline, which is why electrical infrastructure gets more redundancy scrutiny than almost any other system. This course covers the full power chain: utility interconnection, switchgear and transformers, UPS systems (topologies, battery management, maintenance), PDU architecture, and redundancy strategy. Electrical safety and maintenance best practices run through every module — this isn't just architecture theory, it's paired with equipment evaluation frameworks, troubleshooting methodologies, and maintenance scheduling you can apply on the floor. You'll finish with a working understanding of electrical architecture trade-offs, so you can evaluate vendor proposals, troubleshoot power issues faster, and optimize your facility's power chain for both reliability and efficiency.

What you'll cover

  • Utility Interconnection & Distribution
  • Transformers & Switchgear
  • UPS Systems Design & Operation
  • PDU Architecture & Monitoring
  • Redundancy Strategies
  • Electrical Safety & Maintenance Best Practices

Who it's for

Electricians, power engineers, and facilities technicians who work on or specify critical power systems.
