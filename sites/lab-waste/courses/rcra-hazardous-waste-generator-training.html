RCRA Hazardous Waste Generator Training

RCRA hazardous waste generator training for lab and EHS staff.

Labs at large quantity generator institutions must train personnel within six months and review it annually. This eight-hour course covers waste determination, containers, labels, storage, inspections and emergencies.

What you'll cover

  • Listed and characteristic wastes in labs
  • P-listed acute wastes
  • Generator categories
  • Central accumulation areas
  • Inspections and records
  • Emergency procedures

Who it's for

Lab managers, EHS staff and waste handlers.

Related guides

  • Subpart K: the alternative waste rules for academic labs
  • Lab clean-outs and unknown chemicals
