How to read an arc flash label

Every number on the label, and what it means for you.

Equipment like switchboards, panelboards and PDUs that may be worked on while energized should carry an arc flash label.

What you'll see

Label itemWhat it tells you
Nominal voltageThe system voltage
Arc flash boundaryThe distance where incident energy drops to 1.2 cal/cm². Inside it, you need arc-rated PPE
Incident energy at a working distance, or PPE categoryHow much energy could reach you, which sets the PPE
Limited approach boundaryUnqualified people must stay outside unless escorted
Restricted approach boundaryOnly qualified people with shock protection go inside
Date / study referenceWhen the analysis was done

Watch for

  • Labels older than the last system change (NFPA 70E calls for a review at least every 5 years)
  • Very high incident energy. Some equipment shouldn't be worked energized at all
  • Missing labels: stop and ask
