Arc flash PPE categories

Minimum arc rating 4 cal/cm²

Arc-rated long-sleeve shirt and pants or coverall; arc-rated face shield or flash suit hood; hard hat; safety glasses or goggles; hearing protection; heavy-duty leather gloves or rubber insulating gloves with leather protectors; leather footwear as needed.

Minimum arc rating 8 cal/cm²

Arc-rated long-sleeve shirt and pants or coverall; arc-rated flash suit hood, or arc-rated face shield with arc-rated balaclava; arc-rated jacket or rainwear as needed; hard hat; safety glasses or goggles; hearing protection; gloves as for category 1; leather footwear.

Minimum arc rating 25 cal/cm²

Arc-rated flash suit jacket and pants or coverall meeting 25 cal/cm²; arc-rated flash suit hood; arc-rated gloves or rubber insulating gloves with leather protectors; hard hat; safety glasses or goggles; hearing protection; leather footwear.

Minimum arc rating 40 cal/cm²

Arc-rated flash suit jacket and pants or coverall meeting 40 cal/cm²; arc-rated flash suit hood; arc-rated gloves or rubber insulating gloves with leather protectors; hard hat; safety glasses or goggles; hearing protection; leather footwear.

Summarized from the NFPA 70E PPE category method for training. The category for a task comes from your arc flash risk assessment or the NFPA 70E tables, not from this page. Always check the equipment label.
