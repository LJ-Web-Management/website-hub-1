Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Planning Training

A hands-on planning course for building or stress-testing a business continuity plan.

This course complements the Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity comprehensive program with a dedicated, hands-on planning session: building a business continuity plan from scratch, working through ICS-NIMS-aligned emergency management frameworks, and stress-testing the plan against realistic scenarios. You'll leave with a plan structure and documentation approach that holds up under auditor or insurer review, not just a filed-away document.

What you'll cover

  • BC/DR Planning Frameworks
  • ICS-NIMS for Facilities
  • Plan Stress-Testing
  • Audit-Ready Documentation

Who it's for

Operations and risk managers building or updating a formal business continuity plan.
