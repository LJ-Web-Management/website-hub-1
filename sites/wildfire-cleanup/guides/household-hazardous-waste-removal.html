Removing household hazardous waste after a wildfire

The first phase of cleanup, before debris removal.

After large wildfires, a first phase of cleanup often removes household hazardous waste from each property before general debris removal begins. It's usually run by government agencies and their contractors.

Typical items

  • Paints, solvents, cleaners and pool chemicals
  • Pesticides and fertilizers
  • Propane tanks and compressed gas cylinders
  • Batteries, including lithium-ion
  • Ammunition and fuel

How crews work

  1. Assess the property and look for hazards: unstable structures, sinkholes, hot spots
  2. Identify and segregate materials
  3. Package and label for transport
  4. Document what was removed

Training

Crews generally need HAZWOPER training appropriate to their tasks, plus site-specific training. See OSHA 4-Hour HAZWOPER First Responder Awareness Level Training for awareness-level training for others working nearby.
