HAZWOPER terms in English and Spanish

A quick glossary of common site terms for bilingual crews and supervisors.

A shared vocabulary makes briefings faster and safer. Here are common HAZWOPER terms in English and Spanish. Crews may use regional variations, so agree on your own list.
