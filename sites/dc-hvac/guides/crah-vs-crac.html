CRAH vs CRAC: what's the difference?

Two units that look alike on the floor and work very differently.

CRACCRAH
NameComputer room air conditionerComputer room air handler
Cooling sourceIts own refrigeration compressor (DX)Chilled water from a central plant
Heat rejectionCondenser (air-cooled, water or glycol)The chiller plant
Typical siteSmaller rooms and older sitesLarger facilities
Main hazardsRefrigerant pressure, compressor electricalWater leaks, valves, pumps

Troubleshooting differences

CRAC: check refrigerant pressures, superheat and subcooling, compressor amps, condenser airflow and coil condition.

CRAH: check chilled water supply and return temperatures, valve position and actuator, flow, and coil condition.

Both

Filters, belts or EC fan motors, humidifiers, condensate drains and controls.
